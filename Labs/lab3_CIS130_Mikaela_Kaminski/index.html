What Is Matcha?

Matcha is a finely ground green powder made from tea leaves. It has been enjoyed in Japan for centuries and is known for its bright green color and earthy flavor.

Today, matcha is popular around the world and can be used to make tea, lattes, desserts, and other foods.

Benefits of Matcha

  • Rich in antioxidants
  • Provides a unique and refreshing flavor
  • Traditional Japanese beverage
  • Can be enjoyed hot or cold
