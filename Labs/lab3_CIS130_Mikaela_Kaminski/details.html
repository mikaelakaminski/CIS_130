How to Make Matcha Tea

Home Matcha Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon matcha powder
  • 8 ounces warm water

Instructions

  1. Add 1 teaspoon of matcha powder to a bowl or cup.
  2. Pour in a small amount of warm water.
  3. Whisk until the matcha is smooth and free of lumps.
  4. Add the remaining water and whisk until frothy.
  5. Serve and enjoy your matcha tea.

Helpful Tips

Use warm water rather than boiling water for the best flavor. You can also add milk and sweetener if you prefer a matcha latte.
